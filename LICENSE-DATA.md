# Data license

The data under `data/` and `paper/` (the appendix tables and `numbers.tex`) are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/,
by Zhihao Wang, Min Ren, Xia Liang, Gaoyong Li, Ding Ding.

Exceptions: the programs and excerpts of the benchmark cases (`data/lsp_bench/cases/*/project/`
and quoted issue text in `data/lsp_bench/` and `data/lsp_pilot/`) are derived from public issue
reports and repositories and keep the terms of their sources; every case records its sources in
`case.json`. Answers recorded from language servers may quote the source code of the corpus
projects (`data/lsptest/corpus.json` lists them with their repositories); that code keeps the
license of its project.
